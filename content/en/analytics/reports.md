The **Reports** page shows saved Master Chart reports.

![Reports](/en/images/analytics/reports-en.png)

[[delimiter rows=1]]

Reports are divided into two tabs:

- **Shared** — reports available to other users of the **Analytics** module
- **Private** — personal reports available only to their author

To find a report, use the search by name.




### Report Actions

Click the two-dot button on the report card to open the actions menu:

![Report actions](/en/images/analytics/report-edit-en.png)

[[delimiter rows=1]]

- **Edit**
- **Duplicate**
- **Copy Link**
- **Delete**

Duplicating a report opens the window for saving a new report. If the original report is available to other users, its copy can be saved as a private report and changed independently of the original.



### Filters

If necessary, click **Filters** to set additional filtering conditions.

![Filters](/en/images/analytics/reports-filters-en.png)

[[delimiter rows=1]]

After selecting the required values, click **Apply**.

Applied filters are displayed above the list of reports. To remove a single filter, click × next to its name. To reset all filters, click **Clear Filters**.
