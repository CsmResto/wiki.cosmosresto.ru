**Waitlist** helps you save a guest request when the establishment cannot yet confirm a standard reservation. For example, a suitable table is occupied, the required time is not available or placement conditions are still being clarified.

A record on the waitlist does not reserve a table or block time. When a suitable spot becomes available, the record can be converted to a standard reservation.

Waitlist is available in the `Timeline, Floor Plan and Reservations Table` views. On the Timeline and Floor Plan, a separate list is displayed with a counter of active records.

[[info type=custom color=#E06823]]
Waitlist does not guarantee the guest a visit to the establishment. Until the record is converted to a standard reservation, the table and time interval remain free for other reservations.
[[/info]]

## When to Use Waitlist

- Guest submitted a request, but you cannot yet confirm a reservation;
- Guest contacted directly, but no suitable table or time is available now;
- An existing reservation can no longer be saved in its current form, but the guest is ready to consider another time or placement conditions.

## How a Record Gets on Waitlist

1. Converting from a request to a reservation with status `Waitlist`.
2. Creating manually in the **Reservations** section by specifying status `Waitlist`.
3. Changing the status of an existing reservation to `Waitlist`.


## Creating a Record from a Request

<!-- ![Requests](/ru/images/reserve/requests/waitlist3ru.png) -->

[[delimiter rows=1]]

1. Open the request.
2. Select request confirmation.
3. Set status **Waitlist**.
4. Save the changes.

After saving, the request becomes inactive and a record appears on the waitlist.

## Creating a Record Manually

<!-- ![Requests](/ru/images/reserve/requests/waitlist4ru.png) -->

[[delimiter rows=1]]

1. Go to **Reservations** section in any view.
2. Click **Add Reservation**.
3. Fill in guest data.
4. Specify date, time and number of guests.
5. Select a table if necessary, this is not a required field.
6. Select status **Waitlist**.
7. Click **Save**.

## Converting a Reservation to Waitlist

The process is similar to changing a reservation status. After saving, the table is freed and the record is moved to the waitlist.

1. Open the reservation.
2. Change status to **Waitlist**.
3. Save the changes.

## Converting a Record from Waitlist to Reservation

<!-- ![Requests](/ru/images/reserve/requests/waitlist5ru.png) -->

[[delimiter rows=1]]

1. Open a record with status `Waitlist`.
2. Select a table, if it was not previously specified.
3. Change status to `Not confirmed`, `Confirmed` or `In progress`.
4. Save the changes.

After this a standard reservation is created.

## Canceling a Record

1. Open the record.
2. Change status to `Cancelled`.
3. Specify the reason.
4. Confirm the action.

[[info type=custom color=#E06823]]
The reason for cancellation is required. Waitlist cancellation reasons match reservation cancellation reasons. A cancelled record cannot be recovered.
[[/info]]
