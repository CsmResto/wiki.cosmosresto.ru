## Creating a Manual Tag Group

To create a new Manual Tag group:

1. Click **Create**.
2. Fill in the group settings.
3. Click **Save**.

![Create Manual Tag Group](/en/images/manual-tags/tags-group-create-en.png)

[[delimiter rows=1]]

**1. Name**

**2. Locations**

**3. Description**

**4. Editable in Reservations** — allows you to assign and remove tags from this group directly in the reservation system.

**5. Available in Waiter App** — allows you to use tags from this group in the Waiter App.

   - **Assign to Guest** — allows you to assign and remove tags from this group in the Waiter App. This setting becomes available only when **Available in Waiter App** is enabled.

**7. Available in Reservation Widget** — allows you to display tags from this group in the Reservation Widget. When this setting is enabled, you can specify a public group name that will be displayed in the widget.

**8. Reservations Display** — allows you to choose where the group tags will be displayed: in the guest profile, time slot, on the table, in the call widget, in the guest hint, and in the Reservation List.



## Creating a Tag

To add a tag to a group:

1. Click **Add Tag**.
2. Fill in the tag settings.
3. Click **Add**.
4. Click **Save** for the changes to take effect.

![Create Tag](/en/images/manual-tags/tags-create-en.png)

[[delimiter rows=1]]

**1. Name**

**2. Short Name**

**3. Color**

**4. Icon**

**5. Hint Text**


## Editing

To change the settings of a group or its Manual Tags, open the required group, make the changes, and click **Save**.

## Deleting a Manual Tag Group

To delete a Manual Tag group, open the actions menu in the group list or on the group page and select **Delete**.

Confirm the deletion in the window that opens.

When a group is deleted, all tags in the group are deleted and removed from all guests they were assigned to.

## Deleting a Manual Tag

To delete a Manual Tag, open the tag group, remove the required tag from the list, and click **Save**.

After deletion, the tag will be removed from all guests it was assigned to.
