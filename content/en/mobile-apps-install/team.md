**Cosmos Team** - mobile application for logging into shifts, viewing salaries, quests and modifiers.

iOS link: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/uz/app/cosmos-team/id6780405989)

Android link: [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ru.cosmosresto.team)

### Login Instructions:

1. Open the application

2. In the **"Connection"** field enter the value that indicates your establishment

    **How to find out:** Take the part of the link where you open Cosmos in the browser. The required part of the link is indicated before csmresto.ru. For example, for https://demo.csmresto.ru/ the value is **demo**. If you are unsure, the value can be clarified from technical support

3. Click **"Connect"**

4. Enter employee login details

5. Click **"Log in"**

[[info type=custom color=#E06823]]
If you encounter a problem `"Server is temporarily unavailable"` when trying to log in, then most likely the value in the second step was entered incorrectly. For this you need to:

1. Click on the text **"Return to application selection"**
2. In the **"Connect to"** field enter the value that indicates your establishment
3. Click **"Connect"**
4. Enter employee login details
5. Click **"Log in"**

If the problem persists, contact technical support, we will help you figure it out
[[/info]]
