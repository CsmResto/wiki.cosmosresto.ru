To create a reservation, tap **+** at the top of the reservation list. The new reservation form opens.

## Form Fields

1. **Don't Change** - prevents changing the table and the guest in the reservation. All other fields can be changed.
2. **Select Guest** - search for a guest or create a new one. If no specific guest is needed, tap the ghost icon button on the right - the reservation will show **Walk-in Guest** without a name and phone number.
3. **Select Table** - one or several tables. A reservation can be saved without a table, and the table can be selected later.
4. **Date** - use the arrows to go to the previous or next day, or select a date with the calendar icon.
5. **Time** and **Duration** - changed with the **-** and **+** buttons in 15-minute steps. **Reservation Interval** below shows the start and end time of the reservation.
6. **Number of guests** - up to 10 guests are selected by tapping the icon; for a larger party tap **10+** and enter the number manually.
7. **Reservation source**
8. **Comment**
9. **Status** - **Confirmed** by default.

If reservation tags are used in the establishment, the form also has a field for reservation tags.

To save the reservation, tap **Create**.
