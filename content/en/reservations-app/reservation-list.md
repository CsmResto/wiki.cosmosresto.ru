**CosmosResto Reservations** on the phone is an app for working with reservations without a computer or tablet. Installation and login are described in the **[Reservations](https://wiki.cosmosresto.ru/en/mobile-apps-install/reservations/)** article.

After login, the list of reservations for the current date opens. Reservations are grouped by status in the following order: **Not Confirmed**, **Confirmed**, **Started**, **Ended**, **Cancelled**. The number of reservations in a group is shown next to its name.

## Reservation Card

![Reservation Cards](/en/images/reservations-app/reservation-cards-en.png "400")

[[delimiter rows=1]]

A reservation card shows:

- the guest's phone number and name, loyalty card number;
- the table - if the reservation is for several tables, the number of the other tables is shown next to it, for example **+1**. A lock next to the table means that **Don't Change** is on in the reservation: the table and the guest cannot be changed;
- start and end time, number of guests;
- a comment icon if the reservation has a comment;
- the table's minimum spend - how much has already been spent out of the amount that has to be spent at this table, for example **3K/26K DH**;
- the time until the guest arrives.

If another employee is editing the reservation at the moment, the card shows the name of this employee.

To open a reservation, tap its card - see the **[Reservation View](https://wiki.cosmosresto.ru/en/reservations-app/reservation-view/)** article.

## Screen Elements

![Screen Elements](/en/images/reservations-app/reservation-menu-en.png "297")

[[delimiter rows=1]]

1. **Menu** - opens and closes the menu of the app sections. The open section is highlighted with a frame.
2. **[Floor Plan](https://wiki.cosmosresto.ru/en/reservations-app/floorplan/)**
3. **Reservation List**
4. **[Requests](https://wiki.cosmosresto.ru/en/reservations-app/requests/)**
5. **Profile** - see below.
6. **+** - creating a reservation, see the **[Creating a Reservation](https://wiki.cosmosresto.ru/en/reservations-app/reservation-create/)** article.

## Profile

The profile opens from the menu. Here you can turn **Web Push** notifications on or off and select the **Language** and the **Location** you work with. **Lock Screen** locks the app, **Log Out** logs you out of the account.
