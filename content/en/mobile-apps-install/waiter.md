**Cosmos Waiter** - mobile application for a waiter to register orders and view guest information.

iOS link: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/sg/app/cosmos-waiter/id6779239041)

Android link: [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ru.cosmosresto.waiter)

To log into the application, you need to scan a QR code from the cash register.
