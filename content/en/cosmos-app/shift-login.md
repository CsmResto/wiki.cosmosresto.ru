## Geolocation and Photos

Before entering a shift you must:
- Connect to the establishment's Wi-Fi network;
- Enable geolocation;
- Grant the application access to geolocation.

Otherwise, the application will think that you have not yet arrived at the establishment and will not allow you to start a shift.

To start a shift, select your role and take a photo, following simple rules:
- Your appearance must comply with the established internal regulations;
- The photo must be full-length;
- Your face must be visible;
- The lighting must be bright;
- There should be no editing or filters.
