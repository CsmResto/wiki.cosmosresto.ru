An event is an occasion at the venue with a limited number of tickets. Guests buy tickets online through the events widget on the venue's website.

To work with events, open **Events → Events**. The page shows all events of the selected location, split into tabs by status.

## Page Elements

1. **Geo** - the location whose events are shown in the list.
2. **Period** - filters events by start date.
3. **Search** - search by event name. The search starts after you type three characters.
4. **Create** - creates a new event.
5. **Active**, **Drafts**, **Ended** - tabs with events in different statuses.

To open an event, click its row.

## Tabs

- **Active** - published events that have not ended yet. They are shown in the widget, and guests can buy tickets for them.
- **Drafts** - events that are not published yet. They are not shown in the widget, and guests cannot buy tickets for them. A draft may already have sold tickets if a staff member assigned them to guests in advance.
- **Ended** - events whose end date has passed. An event moves to this tab automatically, and ticket sales close.

## Table Columns

By default, the list is sorted by start date, with later events at the top.

- **Tickets Amount** - how many tickets are sold out of all issued tickets. When all tickets are sold, the bar turns green.
- **Cost** - the ticket price and the ticket level name. If the event has several ticket levels, the number of other levels is shown next to it, for example **+1**. For an event that does not require payment, **Free** is shown.
- **Duration** - the time from the start to the end of the event.
- **Created At** - who created the event and when.
- **Last Update** - who last changed the event and when.
