**Call History** is a table view of the reservation module that shows phone calls received through the telephony integration. One row is one call.

The call history helps you:

- handle incoming calls;
- keep track of missed calls;
- assess how many calls end with a reservation.

To open the call history, click the phone icon in the left menu of the reservation module.

![Call History](/en/images/reserve/call-history/call-history1-en.png)

[[delimiter rows=1]]

## Call Info

Click a call row to open the **Call Info** sidebar. It shows the call type, status, time and duration, the guest and the phone number.

If a reservation was created from the call, it is shown in the **Linked Reservation** block. Click the reservation to open it in view mode. The **Changelogs** button in the reservation opens the list of all changes to this reservation.

[[gallery gap=12 layout=carousel]]
![Call Info](/en/images/reserve/call-history/call-history-sidebar1-en.png)
![Linked Reservation](/en/images/reserve/call-history/call-history-sidebar2-en.png)
![Changelogs](/en/images/reserve/call-history/call-history-sidebar3-en.png)
[[/gallery]]

[[delimiter rows=1]]

To open the **Guest Info** sidebar, click the block with the guest's name in the open reservation. It has two tabs:

- **Profile** — the guest's RFM segment, autotags and manual tags, loyalty number, loyalty bonuses, discount, sex and last visit date. You can also add a manual tag to the guest here;
- **Statistics** — the guest's visit statistics.

![Guest Info](/en/images/reserve/call-history/call-history-sidebar4-en.png)

[[delimiter rows=1]]

## Statistics

To open the statistics, click the button in the lower-right corner of the table. The statistics have two tabs: **Calls** and **Conversion**. On both tabs, the data is split into incoming and outgoing calls.

[[gallery gap=12 layout=carousel]]
![Calls](/en/images/reserve/call-history/call-history-stats1-en.png)
![Conversion](/en/images/reserve/call-history/call-history-stats2-en.png)
[[/gallery]]

[[delimiter rows=1]]

The **Calls** tab shows:

- **Total** — the total number of calls;
- **Incoming** — calls that are coming in right now;
- **Accepted**
- **Completed**
- **Missed**
- **Avg Duration** — the average call duration.

The **Conversion** tab shows:

- **Reservation Conversion** — the share of calls that resulted in a reservation;
- **Reservations Created** — the number of reservations created from calls.

## Table Settings

To change the displayed columns, click the table settings button in the upper-right corner.

In the panel that opens, you can:

✔ Select the columns to display  
✔ Change the column order — drag a column in the list by the icon to the left of its name  
✔ Pin the required columns — click the pin icon to the right of the column name. Pinned columns move to the beginning of the table  
✔ Restore the default settings using **Reset to Default**

![Table Settings](/en/images/reserve/call-history/call-history-columns-en.png)

[[delimiter rows=1]]

By default, the table displays the following columns:

- **Call Type** — incoming or outgoing
- **Call Status**
- **Missed Status** — for missed calls: Didn't call back, Didn't get through, Called back or Guest called back
- **Call Time** — the date and time the call started
- **Duration**
- **Guest** — the guest's name and RFM segment
- **Phone**
- **Reservation Status** — the status of the reservation created from the call

## Filters

Click **Filters**, select a parameter on the left, check the required values and click **Apply**.

![Filters](/en/images/reserve/call-history/call-history-filter-en.png)

[[delimiter rows=1]]

You can filter calls by:

- **Call Status** — Incoming, Accepted, Completed, Missed
- **Call Type** — Incoming, Outgoing
- **Missed Status** — Didn't call back, Didn't get through, Called back, Guest called back
- **Reservation Status** — Not Confirmed, Confirmed, Started, Ended, Cancelled, Waitlist
