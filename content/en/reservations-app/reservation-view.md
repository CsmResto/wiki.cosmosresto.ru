To open a reservation, tap its card in the reservation list.

![Opening a Reservation](/en/images/reservations-app/reservation-open-en.png)

[[delimiter rows=1]]

The reservation opens with all its details: guest, table, date, time and duration, number of guests, source, comment and status. The guest's tags are shown under the guest's name. You can change the details and tap **Save**.

![Reservation](/en/images/reservations-app/reservation-view-en.png)

[[delimiter rows=1]]

## Guest Info

To see the guest info, tap the guest in the reservation. The info is split into two tabs: **Profile** and **Statistics**.

The **Profile** tab shows:

- the guest's name and phone number;
- **Autotags** and **Manual Tags** - a manual tag can be added with **Add Tag** or removed with the cross;
- **Loyalty Number**, **Loyalty Bonuses**, **Discount**;
- **Sex** and **Last Visit**;
- the guest's note.

![Guest Profile](/en/images/reservations-app/guest-profile-en.png)

[[delimiter rows=1]]

The **Statistics** tab shows:

- **Visits** - how many times the guest came, cancelled a reservation or did not show up, in percent and in numbers;
- **Last Check** - the button opens the guest's last order;
- **Top Products** - what the guest orders most often, with **Top Modifiers** for them.

![Guest Statistics](/en/images/reservations-app/guest-stats-en.png)

[[delimiter rows=1]]

To replace the guest in the reservation, tap **Change Guest** at the bottom of the screen.
