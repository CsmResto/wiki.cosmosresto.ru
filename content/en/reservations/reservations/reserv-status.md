Status shows the current state of a reservation: whether it is confirmed, if the guest is in the establishment or the reservation was cancelled. You can specify the status in the sidebar when creating or editing a reservation.

**Five reservation statuses are provided:**
1. Not confirmed
2. Confirmed
3. In progress
4. Completed
5. Cancelled

## INDICATORS AND ALERTS

The color of the reservation card helps quickly determine its current status.

### Color Indicators on Timeline:

1. **Yellow** — status "Confirmed"
2. **Orange** — status "Not confirmed"
3. **Green** — status "In progress"
4. **Dark green** — status "Completed"
5. **Pink** — status "Not confirmed", if guest is late
6. **Light blue** — status "Confirmed", if guest is late
7. **Red** — status "Cancelled"

### Additional Alerts:

- **Text alerts for late arrival** — displayed on the reservation card on the timeline from the first minute of guest delay.

- **Text alerts for reservation end** — shows how much time remains until the end of the guest's visit.
